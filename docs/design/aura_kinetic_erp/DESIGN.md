## Brand & Style
The design system positions high-performance fitness management as an elevated, restorative sanctuary. Tailored for premier boutique fitness clubs, wellness retreats, and athletic performance facilities across Cairo, Giza, and Alexandria, the interface strips away the clinical clutter of legacy ERP software in favor of an airy, serene, and responsive atmosphere. 

The aesthetic fuses **Architectural Glassmorphism** with **Atmospheric Minimalism**. Interfaces live on an open, gallery-grade canvas that breathes calmly while delivering ultra-precise operational control. The emotional tone is deliberate, rejuvenating, and commanding:
- **Serenity Meets Precision:** Front-desk booking, trainer allocations, multi-branch revenue tracking, and member physiological telemetry feel weightless rather than taxing.
- **Bi-Directional Luxury:** Complete natural parity between English and Arabic layouts, ensuring right-to-left (RTL) typography exhibits the exact same grace, letterform dynamics, and optical hierarchy as its Latin counterpart.
- **Sculptural Illumination:** Soft organic glows define zones of momentum and vitality, contrasting sharply with an ultra-deep tactile command console for intense operational tasks.

## Colors
The palette balances an ultra-pale mineral background with precise tactical accents and organic bioluminescent gradients:

- **Canvas Foundation:** Dynamic off-white transition anchored from `#F2F2F4` through `#FAFAFB`.
- **Primary / Noir Anchor (`#0A0A0A`):** Pure deep obsidian used for core text hierarchy, authoritative action states, and the docked bottom command drawer.
- **Kinetic Lime Aurora (`#D4F55A` to `#3C5A12`):** Represents athletic propulsion, live facility occupancy, operational efficiency, and revenue delta indicators. Applied with controlled optical glow in dark console contexts.
- **Organic Vitality Glow (`#FF5C9A` to `#FFD1E3`):** Evokes physiological health, heart rate metrics, member milestones, and VIP tiers. Used primarily as a diffused atmospheric radiance behind frosted layers.
- **Glass Shards & Translucencies:** Pure white layers at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` supported by an ultra-thin hairline rim `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` over light areas and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` over dark areas.
- **Egyptian Pound Localization:** Currency metrics explicitly render the `EGP` symbol (or `ج.م` in Arabic) using a muted secondary tone `#7A7E85` to maintain visual focus on financial figures.

## Typography
Typography creates an ethereal, high-fashion editorial feel across complex enterprise data. 

- **Weight Discipline:** Primary statistical readouts and hero headers utilize ultra-light weights (`300` and `200`) paired with tightly tracked negative letter spacing to project sophistication without sacrificing legibility.
- **Bilingual Typographic System:** 
  - **Latin Contexts:** **Outfit** manages large scale headlines and high-visibility financial metrics; **Plus Jakarta Sans** powers dense UI elements, member schedules, and operational logs.
  - **Arabic Contexts (RTL):** Substitute with **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**. Match weights proportionally: weight `300` translates to `300 Light`, `400` to `Regular`, and `600` to `SemiBold`. Ensure optical vertical alignment adjustments of +1px on Arabic inline tags to compensate for baseline variance.
- **Numbers & Currencies:** Tabular figures (`tnum`) enabled by default across all financial records, member session trackers, and capacity gauges.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a flexible 12-column grid system built around sweeping architectural margins that shield the user from cognitive overload.

- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) margins. Main work surface is organized via modular, free-floating glass clusters.
- **Tablet / iPad Pro (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, 16px gutters, 24px margins. Operational drawers adapt into overlay slide-overs.
- **Mobile (375px - 430px):** 4 columns, 12px gutters, 16px margins. Heavy card padding decreases from `2.25rem` to `1.25rem` to prioritize screen real estate while retaining curved corner aesthetics.
- **Floating Island Architecture:** Master navigation docks float `24px` above the bottom screen viewport. Cards within the main grid do not snap to screen edges; they float with dedicated air cushions.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed through atmospheric layers and refractive frosted surfaces rather than traditional muddy dropshadows:

- **Level 1 (Ambient Glass Panes):** Surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` supported by a `24px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`). Outlined by a continuous `1px` translucent boundary `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`. Soft diffuse shadow: `0 12px 40px -10px rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hero Vitrines & Overlays):** Used for highlighted class allocations and member profiles. Backed by organic, low-opacity radial gradient glows:
  - Vitality Glow: `radial-gradient(circle at 80% 20%, rgba(255, 92, 154, 0.18) 0%, rgba(255, 209, 227, 0) 65%)`
  - Performance Glow: `radial-gradient(circle at 10% 90%, rgba(212, 245, 90, 0.22) 0%, rgba(60, 90, 18, 0) 60%)`
- **Level 3 (Command Obsidian Drawer):** Anchored bottom panel in solid `#0A0A0A` elevated with `0 -16px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)`. Accented with a top hairline stroke `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` and an internal reactive glow shifting from `#D4F55A` (operational active) to `#3C5A12` (standby).

## Shapes
Shapes express architectural fluidity and organic wellness ergonomics:

- **Primary Glass Cards:** Styled with deep, continuous corner smoothing using radii between `28px` and `32px`.
- **Control Elements & Navigation Pills:** Exact pill geometry (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) across search fields, filter toggles, floating dock bars, and interactive tags.
- **Micro Hairline Visualizations:** Sparklines, ring completion bars, and biometric frequency dividers maintain strict `1px` to `1.5px` stroke thicknesses with rounded caps (`stroke-linecap: round`).
- **Inner Nested Enclosures:** Nested child containers inside frosted cards step down in radius to `18px - 20px`, maintaining consistent concentric geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic:** Obsidian background `#0A0A0A` with crisp white text, pill-shaped, `16px` vertical padding, `28px` horizontal. On hover, reveals a subtle underlying halo of `#D4F55A`.
- **Vitality Action:** Pill-shaped, gradient-fill of `#FF5C9A` to `#FF8EB6` with white text, utilized exclusively for high-touch member conversion and VIP package renewals.
- **Glass Ghost:** Frosted translucent background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`, hairline outline `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`, and `#0A0A0A` text.

### Floating Pill Navigation
- Suspended `24px` from the bottom edge. Translucent smoked obsidian (`rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.88)`) or milky crystal (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), `32px` blur.
- Active route state framed in a miniature pill with a lime indicator dot (`#D4F55A`).

### Cards & Glass Vitrines
- Frosted base `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`, backdrop blur `24px`, radius `30px`, perimeter stroke `1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`.
- Internal sections are separated by ultra-light dividers: `1px solid rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.05)`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Pill-shaped `52px` height inputs with frosted neutral fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`. Active state produces an outer glow in `#D4F55A` (0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 245, 90, 0.35)).
- Integrated currency inputs prepend or append `EGP` / `ج.م` in a subdued weight with fixed tabular spacing.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes: Soft-square (`8px` radius) custom toggles. When selected, solid `#0A0A0A` base with an accented `#D4F55A` checkmark.
- Radio buttons: Double concentric rings with an outer stroke of `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.15)` and an inner solid dot of `#FF5C9A` when active.

### Micro-Visualizations & Telemetry
- Facility utilization meters use continuous ring trackers with an outer diameter of `44px`, an ultra-thin `2px` track width, and a glowing gradient sweep (`#D4F55A` to `#3C5A12`).
- Financial trends show single-pixel smoothed bezier curves over faint vertical grid guides.
